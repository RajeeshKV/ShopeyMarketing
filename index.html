Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about setting up and owning your store.

Are there really no monthly platform subscription fees?

Yes! Unlike Shopify or other hosted platforms that charge ₹2,000–₹8,000 every single month, Shopey is a one-time ₹10,000 setup. You own your storefront code without recurring platform rentals.

What is required to connect Razorpay for payments?

You only need your verified Razorpay account (Key ID & Key Secret). We configure the payment gateway keys into your environment so you receive money directly into your bank account.

Can I customize the logo, colors, and banner images myself?

Yes. We do the initial setup with your brand identity, and you have complete control inside the Admin Panel to update banners, categories, colors, social links, and legal policies anytime.

Where is the website hosted?

The front end is deployed on ultra-fast edge hosting (Vercel/Cloudflare) and backend services are hosted on robust cloud infrastructure. We guide you through free and economical hosting tiers.
